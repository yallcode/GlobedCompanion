# GlobedCompanion

Adds a voice chat toggle button to the in-game HUD for mobile Globed players.

On PC you can just press a hotkey to mute or unmute your mic. On mobile there's no way to do that mid-level without leaving the game. This mod fixes that by putting a small mic button directly in the HUD, sitting between the percentage counter and the pause button.

## Features

Mic toggle button in the HUD so you can mute/unmute without pausing. The button turns green when your mic is active and goes back to white when muted.

## Requirements

You need Globed installed for this mod to do anything. If Globed is not installed the mod will not load.

## Notes

This is a mobile-focused mod. It works on Android arm32 and arm64. If you are on PC you probably do not need this since hotkeys already work there.
